01 The demo

Tally

A small bank that can never lose or double a cent. Every transfer is written as two lines that cancel out. A payment sent twice after a dropped connection still lands once. The books can be added up again from scratch to prove nothing leaked. Suspicious transfers are flagged after they land, with the evidence attached.

Everything below is a replay of the real system. Nothing on this page talks to a live server.
